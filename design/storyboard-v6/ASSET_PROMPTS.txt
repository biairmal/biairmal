==============================================================================
BIAIRMAL v6 · THE BLOOD MOON LAKE · ASSET PROMPTS (plain text)
==============================================================================

How to use: each asset below has a READY-TO-PASTE PROMPT (the shared style
is already included), a NEGATIVE PROMPT, and EXPORT NOTES for after you generate.
Copy everything between the "--- copy ---" and "--- end ---" lines.

Reference images: design/storyboard-v6/frames/ (b01-b06, map).

==============================================================================
TECHNICAL SPEC (applies to every model)
==============================================================================
- Format: glTF 2.0 binary (.glb), PBR metallic-roughness, one file per asset.
- Units/axes: 1 unit = 1 metre, Y up, the asset faces +Z.
- Pivot: base centre on the ground (y = 0) unless a note says otherwise.
- Textures: base colour (sRGB), normal (OpenGL, +Y), packed ORM (R=AO, G=roughness, B=metalness). Emissive only where noted.
- Texture size: hero 2048 px, props 1024 px, small items 512 px.
- Keep the material and object names given in the export notes; the site looks them up by name.
- Two versions: desktop at the triangle budget, and name-mobile.glb at about 25-30% of it (same UVs and materials).
- No baked lighting, no AO in the albedo, no red tint, no moon colour.
- Avoid transparency except paper and reed cards (use alpha-clip).

2.1 THE RONIN: RONIN.GLB · RIGGED AND ANIMATED
==============================================================================
--- copy ---
Full-body 3D character of a lone Japanese ronin swordsman, 1.75 m tall, standing in a relaxed, ready A-pose. No armour. He wears layered, worn clothing, all in black and charcoal: A long sleeveless outer coat (haori-style vest) to mid-shin, with a ragged, frayed hem, side slits, and a soft rolled hood collar around the neck. A dark kimono with wide sleeves underneath, with a slightly lighter grey under-collar crossing left over right at the chest. Loose dark hakama-style trousers gathered at the shin into spiral cloth leg wraps (kyahan), dark tabi socks and straw waraji sandals. Wrapped forearms and dark fingerless gloves. At the waist, a dark obi with a thick twisted grey rope belt tied in a hanging knot at the front left, a loop of prayer beads (juzu: mostly black, with every ninth bead off-white), and a small cloth pouch at the right hip. A katana and a wakizashi thrust through the belt at the left hip, hilts forward and angled down-back. Black lacquer scabbards, black iron tsuba, hilts wrapped in dark silk cord, and white sageo cords with small tassels. On his head: a wide, shallow, ribbed conical straw hat (kasa) lacquered black, with a slightly drooping brim and thin chin cords. Under it, a black lacquered menpō face mask covering nose, cheeks and chin, with a stern down-turned mouth, a white horsehair moustache and a red-lacquered interior. A dark hood covers the head under the hat, so no skin is visible. Fabric with fine weave, frayed edges, dusty worn patches and realistic fold detail in the normal maps. Matte cloth contrasted with glossy lacquer. Stylised-realistic Japanese Edo-period aesthetic, cinematic and restrained. Physically based materials with neutral, un-tinted albedo. A monochrome palette: black lacquer, charcoal timber, grey stone, off-white paper and silk. Subtle wear and age: worn edges, faint dust, fine surface detail in the normal maps rather than the geometry. Clean, game-ready topology. No text, logos or signatures. No baked lighting, no ambient occlusion in the albedo, no coloured light. Neutral studio presentation on a plain grey background.
--- end ---
EXPORT NOTES:
  - Budget: 45k triangles LOD0 / 12k LOD1 · 4 × 2048 texture sets (body cloth, gear, head, weapons)
  - Rig and animation
  - Humanoid skeleton, Mixamo / Unreal-compatible bone naming (hips, spine ×3, neck, head, arms with fingers, legs with toes).
  - Extra bones:
  - katana and saya, so the sword can be drawn from the scabbard. The katana needs a full steel blade hidden inside the saya.
  - Cloth chains: coat_0…coat_5, six chains of four bones each, hanging around the coat skirt. sleeve_L/sleeve_R, two-bone chains under each sleeve. The engine simulates these live.
  - Skin weights clean at the shoulders, hips and knees, with the cloth weighted to the chains.
  - Animations (30 fps, in place, no root motion):
  - idle (required): a 4–6 s loop. Calm breathing, a subtle weight shift, the left hand resting on the scabbard by the tsuba.
  - iai (optional): ~6 s. The thumb breaks the scabbard seal, a step forward as he draws, a rising diagonal cut from the left hip to above the right shoulder, a hold, a blade flick, then a slow re-sheathe. Mocap preferred.
  - Materials (names): cloth_vest, cloth_kimono, cloth_under, cloth_trousers, cloth_wraps, cloth_obi, rope, bead_dark, bead_light, kasa_lacquer, lacquer_black, lacquer_red (mask interior), horsehair, silk, ito_wrap, iron, blade_steel, seam_light.
  - Light seams: add thin emissive strips (material seam_light, white, emissive 1.0) along:
  - the kasa rim
  - the front edges of the coat
  - the mask's mouth slit
  - the scabbard mouths (koiguchi)
  - They should be hairline-thin, about 1–2 mm.
