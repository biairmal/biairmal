PART 1 · FULL BRIEF FOR AN AI AGENT
==============================================================================
--- copy ---
You are rigging and animating a game-ready 3D character for a scroll-driven
three.js website. Work in Blender 4.x and deliver a single glTF binary (.glb).

INPUT
- File: ronin_lod0_45k.glb. A Japanese ronin swordsman in an A-pose. One
  merged mesh named "output_unwrapped", about 46k triangles. One material,
  "BakedMaterial", with base colour and metallic-roughness textures at
  4096 px. There is no skeleton and no animation.
- The model is normalised to about 1.9 units tall, centred on the origin. In
  glTF axes it is Y-up and faces +Z.
- His katana and wakizashi are modelled fused into the body mesh, thrust
  through the belt at his LEFT hip, hilts forward and angled down-back. There
  is no blade inside either scabbard.

GOAL
The character stands on one spot for the whole film. He never walks. He
needs exactly two things: a calm idle, and one iai-jutsu draw that ends in a
rising diagonal cut. The draw is scrubbed by the page's scroll position, so
it must look good when played slowly, frame by frame, and backwards.

STEP 1 · SCALE, ORIENTATION, PIVOT
- Scale him to 1.75 m tall (1 unit = 1 m).
- Put the origin at ground level, between his feet.
- He faces +Z in glTF (-Y in Blender). Apply all transforms.

STEP 2 · SEPARATE THE KATANA
- Cut the KATANA (the longer sword: hilt, tsuba and scabbard) out of the body
  mesh into its own objects:
  "katana_hilt" (tsuka + tsuba + habaki) and "katana_saya" (scabbard).
  Cleanly cap the holes left in the body and the belt.
- Model a new "katana_blade": a curved single-edged blade, 0.72 m long, about
  3 cm wide at the base, tapering to a kissaki tip, with a ridge line and a
  bright polished-steel material named "blade_steel" (metallic 1.0,
  roughness 0.12). Seat it inside the scabbard so it's hidden at rest.
- Match the baked texture on the separated hilt and scabbard (keep the same
  UVs and material). The wakizashi stays fused to the body.
- If the scabbard can't be separated cleanly, model a matching black lacquer
  scabbard and hide the original under it.

STEP 3 · SKELETON
- A standard humanoid skeleton with Mixamo-compatible names (mixamorig:Hips,
  Spine, Spine1, Spine2, Neck, Head, LeftShoulder, LeftArm, LeftForeArm,
  LeftHand + five fingers with three joints each, RightShoulder ... RightHand
  + fingers, LeftUpLeg, LeftLeg, LeftFoot, LeftToeBase, and the right leg
  likewise).
- Extra bones:
  * "katana": parent = root (not the hand), so the animation can hand it from
    the scabbard to the hand. Its head is at the tsuba, and its +Y axis points
    along the blade toward the tip. The website draws the slash trail along
    this bone's +Y axis, so the axis must be exact.
  * "saya": parent = Hips. It sits at the scabbard mouth, with +Y along the
    scabbard toward its tail. Skin katana_saya to it.
  * Cloth chains (simulated live on the website, so leave them un-animated):
    "coat_0" ... "coat_5": six chains of four bones (coat_N_0 ... coat_N_2,
    then coat_N_end), hanging around the coat skirt from the waist to the
    hem, evenly spaced around the body. Parent = Hips.
    "sleeve_L_0", "sleeve_L_end" and "sleeve_R_0", "sleeve_R_end": two-bone
    chains hanging under each elbow inside the wide sleeves. Parent = the
    upper arm.
- Skin katana_blade and katana_hilt 100% to the "katana" bone.

STEP 4 · WEIGHTS
- Smooth, clean deformation at the shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips and knees.
- The coat skirt below the belt is weighted to the coat chains, blending into
  Hips / Spine above the belt. The sleeve bags are weighted to the sleeve
  chains (about 85%) blending to the arm.
- The kasa (hat), menpō (mask), moustache and hood follow the Head 100%.
- The belt, beads, pouch and wakizashi follow the Hips.
- Hard items must not stretch: hat, mask, sword guards and the beads.
- Test the full range of the iai below. Nothing may pass through the body.
  Watch the right arm against the hat brim when the arm is raised.

STEP 5 · ANIMATIONS (30 fps, in place, no root motion, Y-up)

Clip "idle": 4.0 s (120 frames), seamless loop
- Calm standing ready stance, feet shoulder-width, weight slightly forward.
- Left hand rests on the katana scabbard just behind the tsuba, thumb against
  the guard. Right arm relaxed at his side.
- Slow breathing in the chest and shoulders. A tiny weight shift. The head
  turns a few degrees and back. Subtle and dignified, nothing fidgety.

Clip "iai": 2.6 s (78 frames), plays once and holds its last frame
The timing is exact: the website syncs the camera and the slash effect to
these frames.
- f0–f13 (0.00–0.45 s): from the idle pose, the right hand moves to the
  katana hilt and grips it. The left hand holds the scabbard.
- f13–f21 (0.45–0.70 s): KOIGUCHI. The left thumb pushes the tsuba and about
  3 cm of blade shows. The body settles, knees soften, eyes on the target.
  Frame 21 is the HERO POSE. The website holds this exact frame for a long
  time: from the top of the page and through the whole camera orbit around
  him (the camera sees it from his front-left, his left side, directly behind
  him, his right side, and his front). So this pose must read clearly as
  "about to draw" FROM EVERY ANGLE, especially in silhouette from behind
  against a bright moon. No breathing or movement is added on top of it.
- f21–f31 (0.70–1.04 s): THE DRAW. The front (right) foot steps forward. The
  left hand pulls the scabbard BACK (saya-biki) while the right hand draws
  the blade out, edge up, and the hips start to rotate.
- f31–f34 (1.04–1.12 s): the tip clears the scabbard mouth.
- f34–f40 (1.12–1.34 s): KIRIAGE, the cut. One fast RISING DIAGONAL cut from
  low at his LEFT hip, across the front of his body, to high above his RIGHT
  shoulder, with the arm fully extended at the end. The blade sweeps a clean
  arc in roughly one plane. Hips and shoulders unwind into it, with a slight
  overshoot at the end.
  The website plays f21–f40 only when the orbiting camera arrives in front of
  him, so the whole draw and cut is seen from the front.
- f40–f78 (1.34–2.60 s): ZANSHIN. He holds the finished cut, blade high and to
  the right, body still. A very slow settle and breath. The left hand stays
  at the scabbard on his hip.
- During the whole clip his feet stay planted after the step. No sliding.

Clip "noto" (optional): 3.0 s (90 frames), plays once
- From the last frame of "iai": a small blade flick to the side (chiburi),
  then the tip finds the scabbard mouth and the blade slides slowly home
  (noto), ending in the idle pose.

Motion quality
- Grounded, economical, real kenjutsu. Motion capture or mocap-quality
  keyframes, not floaty game moves.
- The cut must be FAST (0.2 s) and then completely still. The contrast is the
  point.
- It must look correct when scrubbed slowly and played in reverse.

STEP 6 · MATERIALS
- Keep "BakedMaterial" for everything except the new blade ("blade_steel").
- Optional: thin emissive strips (material "seam_light", white, emissive
  strength 1.0, about 1–2 mm wide) along the hat rim and the scabbard mouth.

STEP 7 · EXPORT
- glTF binary (.glb), Y-up, apply modifiers, include the skins and all
  animations as separate named actions ("idle", "iai", "noto"). Use the "NLA
  tracks" or "Actions" export mode, so each clip is exported as its own
  animation.
- Export TWO files:
  ronin.glb: the full mesh, textures downscaled to 2048 px.
  ronin-mobile.glb: the mesh decimated to about 15k triangles, textures at
  1024 px, the same skeleton, weights and animations.
- Don't apply Draco, meshopt or KTX2 compression. I'll compress afterwards.

STEP 8 · VALIDATION (report each item)
- [ ] Height is 1.75 m, the origin is between the feet, and he faces +Z in the
      glTF.
- [ ] Bones exist and are named exactly: katana, saya, coat_0_0 ... coat_5_end,
      sleeve_L_0, sleeve_L_end, sleeve_R_0, sleeve_R_end, and the
      mixamorig humanoid set.
- [ ] The katana bone's +Y points from the tsuba to the blade tip in every
      frame.
- [ ] katana_blade is hidden inside the scabbard in "idle" and in frames 0–21
      of "iai", and fully drawn from frame 34 on.
- [ ] "idle" loops with no pop. "iai" frame 21 is the hero pose and frame 40
      is the end of the cut.
- [ ] Nothing passes through the body or the hat during "iai".
- [ ] Both files open in https://gltf-viewer.donmccurdy.com and play all
      clips.
- [ ] Report the triangle count and the file size of each file.
--- end ---

A Japanese samurai standing still in a calm, ready stance, feet shoulder-width apart, left hand resting on the sword scabbard at his left hip with the thumb on the guard, right arm relaxed at his side. Slow, deep breathing, a very subtle weight shift, the head turning slightly and back. Dignified and patient, minimal movement, seamless loop.

[iai: 2.6 s, 30 fps, plays once and holds. Seen from the front]
--- copy ---
A Japanese swordsman performs an iaijutsu quick-draw from a standing stance. His right hand grips the sword hilt at his left hip and his left thumb pushes the guard to break the seal. He steps forward with the right foot, pulls the scabbard back with the left hand and draws the blade in one motion, then makes a single very fast rising diagonal cut from his low left hip up across his body to high above his right shoulder, arm fully extended. He freezes in that finishing pose and holds completely still, with calm breathing. Realistic kenjutsu, grounded, no flourish, feet planted.
--- end ---

[noto (optional): 3 s, 30 fps]
--- copy ---
A Japanese swordsman, holding his katana raised after a cut, flicks the blade sharply to the side to clean it, then slowly brings the blade across his body and slides it back into the scabbard at his left hip with deliberate control, finishing in a calm standing stance with his left hand resting on the scabbard.
--- end ---

Timing the website relies on, if your tool lets you edit keys after

  iai 0.70 s (frame 21): seal broken, the hero pose, about to draw (held

  iai 1.12 s (frame 34): blade fully out, the cut starts
  iai 1.34 s (frame 40): the cut ends, arm extended up and to the right

==============================================================================
HOW THE WEBSITE USES THE CLIPS (for reference, not for the agent)
==============================================================================
Scroll position, not time, drives the iai:
  1. Top of the page (Hero): "idle" blends into iai f0–f21 as you begin to
     scroll. He reaches the hero pose (f21) quickly and holds it.
  2. The camera circles him: front-left, his left, behind him (the blood moon
     behind him), his right. He stays frozen at f21 the whole time.
  3. The camera arrives at his front: the scroll plays f21–f40, the draw and
     the rising cut. The slash trail is drawn along the "katana" bone.
  4. About text: he holds f40+ (zanshin) while the slash fades.
  5. Approach: the camera passes him down the lantern path. The optional
     "noto" plays here, while he is behind the camera.
  6. Contact: the camera returns behind him. He is back in "idle".
