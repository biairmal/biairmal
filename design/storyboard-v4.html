Design phase · v4 · draft 01

Digital samurai,
now in three dimensions.

A storyboard for the next version of biairmal.github.io. The concept and the story stay the same: the dot-particle samurai, the moon, the cut, the dust, the eclipse and the blood moon. v4 puts that story into a real 3D scene that the scroll moves you through like a camera, instead of a flat canvas sitting behind the page.

心What stays, what changes

KeepDot-particle samurai and moon · moon → crescent → new moon → blood moon arc · kanji section marks (人 道 戦 武 連) · slash and shoji hovers · monochrome with one blood-red accent · Geist + mono + Noto Serif JP · all current copy and content.
ElevateA real WebGL scene: about 60k GPU particles with depth, a lit sphere moon with real phases, a water floor with reflections, fog, bloom and film grain. The camera is driven by scroll. The dust re-forms into each section's kanji. The katana cut slices the page itself.
GuardContent stays as real HTML (SEO, a11y, NDA text). prefers-reduced-motion and low-end devices fall back to the current 2D canvas. 3D is lazy-loaded after first paint. Target is 60fps on desktop and 30+ on mid-range phones.

Prologue: the whetstone

On load · before scroll · new

Frame
Black screen. A single hairline, the edge of a blade, slides across the middle as the 3D assets load. A mono counter reads 064 / 100 · sharpening, and 侍 sits above it.
Motion
At 100 the line flashes white and the screen splits open vertically along it, like a sliding door. The two halves pull apart and show the hero behind them, with the moon already glowing.
Rule
Never longer than 1.6s. If assets are cached it plays as a quick 0.4s cut. Hidden entirely for returning visitors and for reduced motion.
01 · HERO0 – 8%
01構

Stance: iai on still water

Scroll 0 – 8% · camera low, 3° up-tilt

3D
The samurai is a volume of particles with depth, not a flat mask, so it reads from slight angles. It stands on a dark mirror-water plane that reflects it and the moon. The moon is a lit sphere with dot shading and a soft bloom halo. Ash drifts in three depth layers.
Motion
Idle "breathing": the hakama and the hat cord move in wind (curl noise). Slow ripples cross the water. The headline reveals line by line through a diagonal slash mask.
Pointer
The camera orbits ±6° toward the cursor (parallax), and particles near the cursor scatter into ash and re-form, as they do today but now in 3D. The cursor is a small hairline reticle that stretches into a short blade stroke when it moves fast.
UI
Same copy, layout and CTAs. The 一刀両断 vertical rail stays. "Scroll to draw" becomes a tiny hilt icon that slides out of its sheath as you begin to scroll.
02 · THE CUT8 – 14%
02斬

The cut slices the screen

Scroll 8 – 14% · camera dolly-in + 4° roll · signature moment

3D
The draw and the rising cut play as now, scrubbed by scroll. The blade is an emissive line with bloom, and a bright arc trails behind it as it moves.
Motion
At the peak of the cut time drops to quarter speed and the camera pushes in. Then the whole viewport is sliced along the blade's diagonal. The hero (3D and the HTML text) splits into two halves that slide apart a few pixels, with a one-frame white edge and an RGB-split flash. It is the same cut as the SlashText hover, at page scale.
Feel
Snappy, not long: about 0.9s of real time. Scrolling back up reverses it cleanly, so the halves rejoin and the sword goes back in.
03 · DUST14 – 24%
03塵

Dust toward the lens, moon wanes

Scroll 14 – 24% · camera pulls back and rises

3D
The samurai comes apart into dust that blows toward the camera. Close particles grow large and blur with depth of field, so you feel them pass the lens. The moon wanes physically: a light orbits the sphere, so the terminator is real, not a mask.
Motion
This replaces today's empty "quiet stretch" spacer: the scroll is pinned here so the dust has room to play. The water plane sinks away below and the horizon drops out of frame.
Handoff
Not all the dust leaves. One cloud stays in the scene and becomes the "ink" that writes each section's kanji from here on.

About: the dust writes 人

Scroll 24 – 36% · camera slow drift right

3D
The dust cloud flows into a large brush-stroke 人 behind the right column. It is made of the same particles and holds stroke order (left stroke, then right), like it is being written. The crescent rests small in the corner.
Motion
The heading reveals through a slash mask. The fact rows (Based in / Experience / …) draw their top rule left to right, then the text fades in, one row after another. The "{years}+ years" number counts up.
System
Kanji morph is the core device for the middle sections: 人 → 道 → 戦 → 武 → 連. Each section's kanji re-forms from the last one as you scroll between them.

The path: a camera walk

Scroll 36 – 50% · section pinned · camera trucks forward

3D
The flat timeline becomes a real path: one glowing line on a dark ground that runs off toward the horizon, with the four diamond markers set along it. 道 hangs in the sky ahead.
Motion
The section pins while you scroll and the camera walks the path. As each marker passes, it lights up, its card (Plan / Build / Ship / Sustain) comes forward, and the last one fades back. A progress hairline runs along the bottom.
Mobile
Not pinned. The path turns vertical, as today, and the markers light up as they reach the middle of the screen.

Work: each row is a blade

Scroll 50 – 66% · camera holds, low motion

Idea
The accordion stays as real HTML and keeps <details>. Each row's divider is a sheathed blade lying flat. Hover draws it a few centimetres out of its saya with a hiss of light along the edge. Open draws the blade fully across the row, and the panel opens along the cut.
Motion
When a row opens, Problem, Approach and Result fade in one after another. Result numbers animate (40–80k → 7.6k lines, 382 → 280 man-days). The open row's number turns blood-red, as now.
3D
Restrained on purpose, because this is the reading section. 戦 floats faint behind the list, and the background stays calm so the NDA write-ups read clearly.

Toolkit: the tsuba ring

Scroll 66 – 76% · camera tilts down onto the ring

3D
A sword guard (tsuba) made of dots sits at the centre. The tool names orbit it in 3D rings, one ring per group. Names on the near side are large and bright and those on the far side fade, which gives the depth.

08 · ECLIPSE76 – 86%
08蝕

Eclipse: the turn to red

Scroll 76 – 86% · camera drifts up toward the moon

3D
The crescent wanes to a new moon, which gets a faint corona ring like a real eclipse. Then the shadow passes off the other side and leaves a full blood moon. The kanji dust goes dark and then lights red.
Grade
The whole scene's colour grade moves a few percent toward red: fog, grain and ash. The accent dot, focus rings and slash lines in the UI shift from white to blood. It should feel like the night changed, not like a theme switch.
09 · CONTACT86 – 100%

Contact: the second samurai

Scroll 86 – 100% · wide shot, god rays toward camera

3D
The dust gathers into a second samurai with the sword sheathed. He faces a small blood moon whose light streams toward the viewer as volumetric rays. Red ash falls through the rays.
Payoff
Hovering the email makes the samurai's hand move to the hilt (anticipation). Clicking plays a red slash across the email (SlashText in blood) before the mail app opens. The arc closes: you came in on a draw and you leave on a promise of one.
Footer
As now. The live Jakarta clock ticks with a tiny pulse, and "Back to top" plays the scene in reverse at 4× speed back to the stance.

型Global systems

SystemDesign
CursorA hairline reticle. When it moves fast it stretches into a short blade stroke along the motion. Over links it becomes a small ring. Hidden on touch.
Page transitionsHome ↔ /blogs: a diagonal ink wipe in the same direction as the cut. The 3D scene stays mounted and only the camera moves to a quiet "reading" pose for /blogs.
Light modeSumi-e: washi paper grain, ink-black particles, a pale grey moon and a vermilion hanko red as the accent. The same scene with a different look, not a separate design.
Sound optionalOff by default, with a toggle in the header. Wind bed, a sheath slide at the draw, a single "shing" at the cut, a low taiko at the blood moon.
Reduced motionNo pinning and no camera moves. Each scene shows as a still key frame, cross-fading between sections. The current 2D canvas is a valid fallback.
PerformanceThree device tiers set by a quick GPU probe: 60k / 25k / 2D fallback particles. Three.js is code-split and loaded after the HTML paints. The scene pauses off-tab, and the hero text is never blocked by WebGL.
